Threshold, such as splitting a business, structuring its ownership or control,
moving work or revenue to another entity, or shifting revenue or funding
between financial years, is disregarded, and the revenue of the persons and
entities in that arrangement is counted together.

7.1. The Revenue Threshold is reached on the first day of a Financial Year
if Annual Revenue for the preceding Financial Year was at least the Revenue

Year reaches the Revenue Threshold. Once reached, it remains in effect until
the end of a Financial Year in which revenue, counted as for Annual Revenue,
stays below the Revenue Threshold. While it remains in effect, you are above
the Revenue Threshold.

under the EUPL-1.2 every Pill Engine Modification contained in, or used to

Revenue Threshold, publish each further such modification no later than the
