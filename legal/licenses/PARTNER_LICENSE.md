# Pill Engine Partner License - Summary of Terms

## Overview

This page summarizes the Partner License. It is not a license or an offer:
a Partner License exists only as a written agreement signed by the Partner
and the Licensor.

A Partner License is not a separate license. It builds on the
[Community License](COMMUNITY_LICENSE.md) and changes only what the signed
agreement states. Its definitions are therefore inherited from the
Community License, version 1.0: capitalized terms on this page and in a
signed agreement, such as User Project, Pill Engine Modification and Annual
Revenue, have the meaning given there, unless the agreement defines them
differently.

The Partner License is a paid agreement that lets you keep covered Pill
Engine Modifications private at any revenue. It is intended mainly for
Users whose Annual Revenue reaches the Community License's Revenue
Threshold, but any User may ask for one.

It is always a choice, never a requirement. Above the threshold you may
instead stay on the Community License and publish your Pill Engine
Modifications, or use Pill Engine under the [EUPL-1.2](EUPL-1.2_LICENSE.txt)
licensing route. See the main [`LICENSE`](../../LICENSE) file.

Partner funding is meant to support the further development of Pill Engine,
which stays free for everyone. Once the planned non-profit foundation,
independent of any company, exists, the funding will go to it (see
[`GOVERNANCE.md`](../GOVERNANCE.md)).

## Rights granted

- You may keep your Pill Engine Modifications private at any revenue.
  The publishing duty in section 7 of the Community License does not apply.
- Everything the Community License allows continues to apply.
- The license covers you and the User Affiliates named in the agreement.
- You may keep selling and updating User Projects released during the term
  after it ends, on the terms set in the agreement.
- If agreed, the license may also include warranties, a different
  attribution notice or permission for a Pill Engine Redistribution.
- The signed agreement identifies the covered User Affiliates, Pill Engine
  Modifications and versions, and any exclusions. Future modifications are
  covered only if the agreement expressly includes them or the parties add
  them by signed written amendment.

## Limitations

- Third-party components are not covered and stay under their own licenses.
- Only code the Licensor owns or may sublicense under the contributor
  agreements or section 7.3 of the Community License is covered. Before
  signing, the Licensor states in writing which code is covered.
- A Pill Engine Redistribution, such as a fork or a standalone engine, is
  permitted only if the agreement says so, and only within what the
  contributor agreements allow. The same applies to presenting a Pill Engine
  Modification as the official Pill Engine.
- No rights in the Pill Engine name or logos are granted beyond what the
  agreement states.
- The license gives no seat or vote in the Pill Engine Project's governance.

## Indicative pricing

The Partner License fee is an annual amount based on your Annual Revenue
band. It is not a share of revenue from any User Project, but may change at
renewal if your Annual Revenue band changes. Fees are invoiced annually in
advance; the signed agreement states any applicable taxes or proration.

The current indicative annual fee for each band is published on the
[Partner License page](https://docs.pillengine.org/guide/other/partner-license)
in the Pill Engine website.

A change of band applies from the next renewal of the agreement.

The first 10 Partners to sign and pay under a Partner License receive a 50%
discount on the applicable annual fee for the first 4 years. Their
undiscounted fee bands are locked at the rates in effect when they sign.

The published fees are an invitation to discuss terms, not an offer. The fee
in a signed agreement is the one that applies, and the published pricing may
change.
